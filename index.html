    How to play

    Pick up to five cards and play them as a poker hand. Its chips × mult is added to your score.

    Reach the target within four hands. Discards swap cards without scoring.

    After each blind, take a joker. Only the cards that make the hand score, and jokers act on those.

    Play poker hands to beat each blind.
